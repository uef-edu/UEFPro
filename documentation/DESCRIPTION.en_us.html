UEF Pro is the corporate typeface of UEF (University of Economics and Finance, Ho Chi Minh City), designed for the university's websites, applications, documents and printed materials.

The letterforms are clean, modern and straightforward, with a classic touch of humanist grotesque. Vietnamese diacritics are carefully designed for a smooth reading experience on screen and in print. The family comes with 9 weights from Thin to Black, matching italics, and variable font files. OpenType features include tabular figures (tnum), slashed zero (zero), a single-storey a (ss01), fractions, superscripts and subscripts.

UEF Pro is a fork of Be Vietnam Pro by Lam Bao, Tony Le and Vietanh Nguyen (Yellow Type Foundry), licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
